# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful and harassment-free environment for everyone who participates in FDE Mastery, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, religion or sexual orientation.

This policy is based on the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, considerate and constructive.
- Give and accept feedback professionally.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code and decisions rather than individuals.
- Respect different experience levels and communication styles.
- Take responsibility, apologize and learn from mistakes.
- Protect private information and respect project confidentiality.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, inappropriate sexualized language or imagery, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information without permission are unacceptable.

Threats, sustained unwanted contact and other conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe are not acceptable.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this standard. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that violate this Code. Maintainers may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when warranted.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to **hello@tinlance.com** with the subject `FDE Mastery Code of Conduct`. Include relevant links, dates and context. Do not use a public issue for sensitive reports.

The maintainer targets acknowledgement within five business days where practicable, will review reports impartially, protect reporter confidentiality to the extent possible, and communicate an appropriate outcome. Retaliation is not acceptable.

## Scope

This Code applies within project spaces and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.
